Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

In short: Namazım does not collect any data from you. There are no accounts, servers, ads, analytics or third-party trackers. The app does not even have internet permission. Whatever you record stays on your phone.

This policy covers the Android app “Namazım – Mevsimler Değişir” (package name com.kadir.namazim), developed and published by the individual developer Kadir Karaoğlu.

What is kept on your phone

The following is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. It is not sent to us or to any other app.

  • Your prayer records: which prayers you marked as prayed on which day, and your streak.
  • Your chosen location: only the province and district name and the coordinate of that district's centre. Your phone's exact location is not stored.
  • Special-period days: the start and end date, if you use this feature.
  • Your settings: streak tolerance, vibration, widget text, calculation method and similar choices.
  • Scheduling and error log: technical records showing whether the widget updated on time. Kept for the last 7 days; older entries are deleted automatically.

Location

To calculate prayer times the app needs to know your district. You can pick it yourself from the list; in that case location permission is never requested.

If you tap “Find my location”, the app asks only for approximate location. Location is read only when you ask, when the app is opened, or when the time zone changes; location is never used in the background. The reading is converted on the phone, without internet, to the nearest district centre, and only that district is stored. No location history is kept.

Prayer times are calculated on the phone using the method of the Presidency of Religious Affairs of Türkiye (Diyanet). The app does not connect to any service for prayer times.

App permissions

  • Approximate location: only for “Find my location”.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so the widget changes exactly at prayer-time transitions. If you do not grant it, the app still works and changes may be a few minutes late.
  • Run at startup: to rebuild the widget's schedule after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for a short vibration when you tap “prayed” (can be turned off in settings).

In addition, Android libraries add technical permissions for background work. None of them gives the app internet access.

Backup

Android Auto Backup is enabled. If Google backup is on for your phone, the app's data (your records, chosen district, special-period days and settings) is backed up to your own Google account, encrypted with your screen lock, and can be restored on a new phone. We cannot see this backup; it is governed by Google's terms. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Backup and data → Delete all my data deletes everything. Uninstalling the app also deletes all its data on the phone. Since we hold none of your data, there is nothing else for us to delete.

Feedback

You can send comments and bug reports through Google Play reviews. Reviews are public and governed by Google's terms; we recommend not including personal information in them.

If you e-mail us, we receive only what you send (your address, your message and anything you attach yourself). We use it only to reply to you and fix problems, we do not share it, and we do not keep it longer than needed. Incoming e-mails may be read and summarised by an AI tool that helps with development. Your prayer records and special-period information are never added automatically.

Payments

There is currently nothing paid in the app, and there are no ads. If an optional support subscription is added in the future, payments will be handled by Google Play; your card and payment details will not reach us.

Children

The app is designed for ages 13 and up and does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page and the date above will change.
